# YSJ submission note

This version is targeted to the Young Scientists Journal. It uses British English and Chicago author-date citations, as requested by the journal's public submission guidance.

## Central positioning

The paper is presented as a seven-point local sensitivity study, not as a full profile-likelihood constraint or posterior analysis. Its main contribution is the separation of mechanism-level response, cosmological compensation, endpoint validation, and complexity-adjusted interpretation.

## Claims intentionally not made

- No global IDM-DR optimum
- No confidence interval or posterior constraint
- No statistical significance for the sub-unit chi-square difference
- No resolution of the Hubble or S8 tensions
- No improved fit to direct growth data

## Recommended article type

Original Research.
